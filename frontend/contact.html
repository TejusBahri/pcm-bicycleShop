About PCM Bicycle Shop

Welcome to PCM Bicycle Shop, your premier destination for quality bicycles and exceptional service. We're passionate about cycling and committed to helping you find the perfect ride for your needs.

Our Services

  • Professional bike fitting and sizing
  • Expert maintenance and repairs
  • Custom bike builds and modifications
  • Cycling advice and recommendations
  • Group rides and community events
  • Financing options available

Why Choose Us?

With over 15 years of experience in the cycling industry, our certified technicians and cycling enthusiasts are here to provide you with personalized service and expert advice. We carry only the highest quality brands and stand behind every product we sell.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer financing options?

Yes, we offer flexible financing options to help make your dream bike more affordable. Contact us for details about our current financing programs.

What brands do you carry?

We carry premium brands including Trek, Specialized, Giant, Cannondale, and many more. We carefully select each brand for quality and reliability.

Do you provide maintenance services?

Absolutely! Our certified technicians provide comprehensive maintenance and repair services for all types of bicycles.

Can I test ride bikes?

Yes, we encourage test rides! We have a safe area for test rides and can help you find the perfect fit and feel for your new bike.
